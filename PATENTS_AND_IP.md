## 🔬 Detailed Invention Disclosures

### 1. PG-IP-001: Tri-Paradigm Clinical MoE & Popperian Falsifiability Router
* **Field of Invention**: Healthcare Artificial Intelligence, Clinical Decision Support (CDS), Evidence Grading.
* **Abstract**: A system and method for dynamic multi-paradigm medical reasoning combining Western Evidence-Based Allopathy, Chronobiology/Metabolic Medicine, Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM), and Ayurvedic Medha Sakti. The router evaluates candidate clinical assertions through a Popperian null-hypothesis ($H_0$) statistical significance filter ($p < 0.05$) and assigns Oxford CEBM Levels (1–5) and Cochrane RoB 2 risk-of-bias scores before rendering to the clinician.
* **Representative Claim Elements**:
  1. An algorithmic routing matrix that parses patient vitals, symptoms, and laboratory biomarkers into multi-lens diagnostic vectors.
  2. A null-hypothesis verification module that computes $z$-scores and $p$-values against empirical population baselines.
  3. A defensive filter that automatically injects a `skepticalWarningNotice` whenever clinical evidence fails the $p < 0.05$ threshold or exhibits high risk of bias.

### 2. PG-IP-002: Client-Side WebGPU Bio-Signal & Tremor FFT Spectrum Engine
* **Field of Invention**: Digital Health, Computer Vision, Optical Neuromuscular Diagnostics.
* **Abstract**: A zero-cloud, client-side apparatus for optical contactless biometrics executing within standard web browsers via WebGPU compute shaders. The system extracts green-channel chromatic variations from facial regions to derive remote photoplethysmography (rPPG) cardiac inter-beat intervals (IBI), while simultaneously executing fast Fourier transforms (FFT) on fingertip landmark coordinates to differentiate resting Parkinsonian tremor (4–6 Hz) from postural Essential tremor (8–12 Hz).
* **Representative Claim Elements**:
  1. A WebGPU WGSL shader pipeline executing on user edge devices without video frame upload.
  2. A temporal peak detection filter with adaptive bandpass frequency isolation (0.75 Hz – 3.5 Hz for pulse, 3.0 Hz – 15.0 Hz for motor tremor).
  3. Real-time confidence scoring based on optical signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) and ambient illuminance.

### 3. PG-IP-003: Actuarial Clinical Game Theory & Adherence Rebate Mechanism
* **Field of Invention**: Health Economics, Value-Based Care, Algorithmic Behavioral Incentives.
* **Abstract**: A computational game-theoretic mechanism modeling the payer-patient interaction as a bi-level Stackelberg game. The system computes the subgame-perfect Nash equilibrium rebate $r^*$:
$$\Pi_{\text{payer}}(r) = (1 - p_{\text{adhere}}(r)) \cdot C_{\text{hosp}} + p_{\text{adhere}}(r) \cdot (C_{\text{prevent}} + r) + \Delta \text{SharedSavings}$$
Where financial micro-rebates are dynamically adjusted to maximize ACO shared savings, prevent Medicare Part B/D IRMAA cliff penalties, and elevate NCQA HEDIS Star ratings.
* **Representative Claim Elements**:
  1. A utility optimization function calculating patient cost-of-adherence vs. financial incentive.
  2. Dynamic integration with CMS Medicare MSSP benchmark targets and quality bonus thresholds.
  3. Direct cryptographic allocation of calculated rebates to HSA/FSA health spending accounts.
