# Contributor Covenant & Geek Feminism Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, clinical advisors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free and empowering experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, neurodiversity, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and psychologically safe community. We explicitly embrace the principles of **Geek Feminism**, prioritizing equity, dismantling technical gatekeeping, and ensuring safe participation for marginalized voices in open-source health technology.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

*   **Anti-Gatekeeping & Non-Credentialing**: Welcoming contributors of all skill levels without demanding proof of technical or clinical "credentials" or engaging in pedantic testing ("well-actually-ing").
*   **Valuing Non-Code Labor**: Recognizing clinical research, medical accuracy review, documentation, translation, accessibility auditing, and community moderation as first-class, equal contributions alongside code commits.
*   **Demonstrating Empathy & Psychological Safety**: Validating lived experiences, giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, and maintaining patience with learners.
*   **Accepting Responsibility**: Apologizing sincerely to those affected by our mistakes, taking corrective action, and learning from the experience.
*   **Focusing on Community Equity**: Prioritizing accessibility, safety, and systemic inclusion over individual ego or meritocratic elitism.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

*   **Harassment & Unwelcome Attention**: The use of sexualized language or imagery, unwelcome sexual advances, or persistent uninvited contact.
*   **Microaggressions & Elitist Gatekeeping**: Dismissive comments, condescending explanations, misgendering, deadnaming, or questioning someone's right to participate based on their background.
*   **Trolling & Attacks**: Insulting or derogatory comments, sealioning, or personal/political attacks.
*   **Doxxing & Privacy Violations**: Publishing others' private information (such as physical or email addresses) without explicit consent.
*   **Retaliation**: Threatening or penalizing anyone who reports a Code of Conduct incident in good faith.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate, prompt, and fair corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct, and will communicate clear rationale for moderation decisions.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces (including GitHub repositories, issue trackers, discussions, chat channels, and pull requests) and whenever an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.
