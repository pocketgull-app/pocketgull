## 1. System Classification & Legislative Scope

PocketGull qualifies as a **High-Risk AI System** under **Article 6(2)** and **Annex III** of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 because it provides clinical risk evaluation, patient triage recommendations, and care plan strategy suggestions for healthcare professionals.

While PocketGull qualifies as a Non-Device Clinical Decision Support tool in the United States under **FDA 21 CFR §520(o)**, the European Union regulatory framework mandates harmonized conformity for all automated clinical reasoning tools deployed to clinicians evaluating EU residents.

## 2. Harmonized Requirements Conformity Matrix (Chapter III, Section 2)

### Article 9: Risk Management System
* **Continuous Clinical Risk Assessment**: Every clinical suggestion generated by the AI undergoes automated validation against deterministic red-flag criteria (stroke, acute coronary syndrome, severe hypoxemia) before displaying AI recommendations.
* **Epistemic Uncertainty Demarcation**: Implements automated **Welch's $t$-test** against population null hypotheses ($H_0$). If $p \ge 0.05$, the system triggers a mandatory `skepticalWarningNotice` alerting the clinician to insufficient statistical power.
* **Contraindication Interception**: Hardcoded contraindication algorithms cross-reference clinical posology, renal function (eGFR), and drug interactions prior to presenting care strategies.

### Article 10: Data & Data Governance
* **Cohort Independence (Zero Data Leakage)**: Training, validation, and benchmarking of clinical prediction backbones (e.g. SPRINT Cardiometabolic CDS, RSNA Knee Vision) strictly enforce `GroupKFold(n_splits=5)` grouped by `patient_id`. Zero patient observations leak across folds.
* **Demographic Representativeness**: Validation cohorts reflect multi-ethnic clinical diversity (e.g. SPRINT cohort: 35.6% Female, 29.9% Black, 10.5% Hispanic across 102 clinical sites).
* **Bias & Mitigation**: Asymmetric Loss (ASL) with $\gamma_- = 4.0, \gamma_+ = 1.0$ is utilized to address severe clinical class imbalance without biasing against rare pathology.

### Article 11: Technical Documentation & Software Provenance
* **Comprehensive Technical Dossier**: Documented across [ARCHITECTURE.md](file:///c:/Users/philg/Pocketgull/pocketgull/ARCHITECTURE.md), [THREAT_MODEL.md](file:///c:/Users/philg/Pocketgull/pocketgull/THREAT_MODEL.md), and [COMPLIANCE.md](file:///c:/Users/philg/Pocketgull/pocketgull/docs/compliance/COMPLIANCE.md).
* **Supply Chain Provenance**: Automated **CycloneDX 1.6** and SPDX 2.3 SBOM manifests track all dependencies. Container images carry cryptographic build provenance attestations under SLSA Level 3 (`actions/attest-build-provenance`).

### Article 12: Record-Keeping & Event Logging
* **Immutable Audit Trail**: All clinical transactions, emergency overrides, and diagnostic triage actions generate immutable, timestamped SHA-256 digital attestation seals (`IIncidentForensicSnapshot`).
* **Non-Repudiation**: Clinician identity, decision timestamp, and input telemetry hashes are recorded and preserved client-side in encrypted IndexedDB archives.

### Article 13: Transparency & Provision of Information to Deployers
* **Clinical Grounding & Literature Disclosure**: Every AI recommendation explicitly surfaces:
  1. The exact PubMed / DOI citation grounding the recommendation.
  2. The Oxford Centre for Evidence-Based Medicine (OCEBM 2011) evidence level (Levels 1–5).
  3. Cochrane Risk of Bias 2 (RoB 2) ratings across 5 bias domains.
* **Model Card Transparency**: Model versions, intended use, contraindications, and known performance limits are rendered directly in the clinical interface.

### Article 14: Human Oversight (Human-in-the-Loop Architecture)
* **Non-Autonomous Execution Invariant**: PocketGull is strictly a co-pilot. The AI engine is programmatically barred from executing orders, issuing prescriptions, or sending notifications to patients without explicit, affirmative clinician vetting.
* **Step-Up Authentication for Overrides**: Overriding safety filters or modifying controlled medication dosages requires physical **FIDO2 / WebAuthn hardware passkeys (NIST SP 800-63-3 AAL-2)**. Spoken voice commands are strictly an interaction modality, never an authentication bypass.
* **Mandiant Dual-Custody Approval**: Bulk exports (>50 records) or financial actions $\ge \$500$ require dual distinct authenticated clinical/executive roles (`requestorRole !== authorizerRole`).

### Article 15: Accuracy, Robustness & Cybersecurity
* **Empirical Validation Standards**:
  * SPRINT Cardiometabolic CDS: Validation AUROC **0.942**, Sensitivity 91.8%, Specificity 95.4%, Brier Calibration Score 0.042.
  * RSNA Knee Vision Engine: Validation AUROC **0.928**, Sensitivity 89.5%, Specificity 94.1%, Brier Calibration Score 0.061.
* **Cybersecurity Defense Baseline**:
  * **Mozilla HTTP Observatory**: 145 / 100 (Grade A+) with CSP3 nonces and `'strict-dynamic'`.
  * **NIST SP 800-90A**: Hardware CSPRNG entropy enforced; zero `Math.random()`.
  * **HIPAA Safe Harbor**: All 18 identifiers stripped at process boundaries.
  * **Prompt Injection Resilience**: OWASP LLM01 defenses with structural prompt partitioning and Unicode zero-width stripping.

## 3. Post-Market Monitoring (PMM) & Vigilance (Article 72)

3. **Continuous Incident Triage**: Clinical anomalies or unexpected model behavior are reviewed by the Data Protection Officer (`dpo@pocketgull.app`) within 48 hours.
