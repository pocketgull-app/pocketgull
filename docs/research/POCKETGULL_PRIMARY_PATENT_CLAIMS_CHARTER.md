## 1. Field of the Invention
The present invention relates generally to medical informatics, real-time biophysical signal processing, distributed clinical decision support systems (CDSS), and artificial intelligence verification. More specifically, it relates to methods, apparatuses, and non-transitory computer-readable media for autonomously validating AI clinical inferences via statistical null-hypothesis testing, extracting non-invasive biometric telemetry via client-side GPU shaders without video cloud egress, optimizing patient chronic disease adherence via algorithmic Stackelberg game theory, binding hardware biometric stylus dynamics to cryptographic advance directives, and reconciling disparate medical paradigms into mathematically bounded care strategies.

---

## 2. Background of the Invention & Prior Art Deficiencies
Existing clinical artificial intelligence and telehealth platforms suffer from several fundamental technological and architectural limitations:
1. **Unchecked Stochastic Hallucinations**: Large Language Models (LLMs) output convincing medical text based purely on next-token probability, lacking native mechanisms to compute statistical significance against demographic null distributions or discount findings based on clinical trial risk-of-bias (e.g., Cochrane RoB 2).
2. **Severe Privacy Breaches in Optical Telehealth**: Traditional remote photoplethysmography (rPPG) and video-based vital sign estimation systems stream raw, uncompressed patient facial and bodily video to cloud server farms, creating massive HIPAA/GDPR data leakage vectors and violating Safe Harbor §164.514.
3. **Inelastic Adherence Models**: Traditional patient engagement solutions rely on static reminders or flat gamification points, failing to model the economic utility trade-off between patient effort and insurer/ACO hospitalization cost savings.
4. **Fragile Digital Consent Mechanisms**: Existing electronic signature systems capture flat 2D raster bitmaps or static timestamps that can be easily forged, pasted, or deepfaked, lacking dynamic kinematic sensor binding (pressure, tilt, sample jitter).
5. **Siloed Allopathic Monopolies**: Modern EHR systems strictly enforce single-paradigm allopathic coding, unable to safely evaluate or cross-reference complementary traditions (Traditional Chinese Medicine, Ayurveda) for botanical-pharmaceutical CYP450 metabolic contraindications.
