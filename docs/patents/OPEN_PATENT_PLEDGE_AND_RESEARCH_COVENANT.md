## 4. Statutory Prior Art Bar & Defense Against Third-Party Appropriation

### 4.1 Absolute Prior Art Bar Under 35 U.S.C. § 102(a)(1)
Under the America Invents Act (AIA), a person is not entitled to a patent if the claimed invention was patented, described in a printed publication, or otherwise available to the public before the effective filing date of the application.

All specifications, mathematical formulations, WebGPU WGSL shaders, 3D procedural geometries, and algorithmic methods checked into public git commits, published in repository documentation, or rendered in the PocketGull user interface constitute **non-confidential, public, timestamped statutory prior art**.

### 4.2 Protection Against Bad-Faith Patent Registration
* **No Third-Party May Patent This Work**: If any external party, corporation, or laboratory attempts to copy, re-brand, or file patent applications claiming the inventions disclosed herein (or trivial/obvious variants thereof), the public, dated disclosure of these materials establishes an **absolute bar** under:
  * **35 U.S.C. § 102** (Lack of Novelty / Anticipation)
  * **35 U.S.C. § 103** (Obviousness to a Person Having Ordinary Skill in the Art)
* **Duty of Disclosure & Inequitable Conduct (35 U.S.C. § 115, 37 CFR 1.56)**: Any patent applicant who submits an application to the USPTO is under a strict legal duty of candor. Naming oneself as the inventor of work copied from another constitutes fraud on the USPTO, rendering any resulting patent permanently unenforceable.

### 4.3 USPTO Third-Party Preissuance Submissions (35 U.S.C. § 122(e) / 37 CFR 1.290)
PocketGull maintains an active IP monitoring apparatus. Pursuant to 35 U.S.C. § 122(e) and 37 CFR 1.290, PocketGull and any member of the public have the statutory right to submit these published specifications, commit timestamps, and documentation directly into the examination file of any published third-party patent application that attempts to claim these technologies. Examiners are legally obligated to evaluate these submissions during prosecution.

## 5. Freedom to Operate & Prior Art Demarcation ("Vice Versa")

To avoid stepping on legitimate third-party rights while maintaining freedom to operate:
1. **Overcoming *Alice* § 101 via Concrete Physical Telemetry**: Unlike abstract predictive algorithms or generic EHR alert pop-ups (which are subject to rejection under 35 U.S.C. § 101 and *Alice Corp. v. CLS Bank*), PocketGull's claims are explicitly tied to physical transformations:
   * Client-side WGSL compute shaders decomposing optical chrominance from camera sensor feeds.
   * Closed-loop state machine telemetry rate transitions (e.g., dynamically accelerating from 15-minute routine polling to 2-minute emergency frequency upon epistemic abstention).
   * Multi-objective constrained optimization bounded by biophysical CYP450 enzyme kinetics.
2. **Prior Art Benchmarking (Epic Sepsis Model - ESM)**: PocketGull’s finite-sample conformal prediction and epistemic abstention framework directly addresses and demarcates itself from the severe 88% false alarm rate and 0.63 AUROC deficiencies of uncalibrated point-probability classifiers (*Wong et al., 2021 JAMA Intern Med*).
