## 🌟 Our Philosophy: "Live the Medicine"

At Pocket-Gull, we believe that people building healthcare, clinical intelligence, and longevity tools should experience **vitality, sovereignty, and deep personal growth** through their work. 

We reject burnout culture. We don't believe in late-night crunch or pointless corporate theater. Instead, we practice **regenerative work**:
* **Circadian-First Autonomy**: Work asynchronously when your cognitive focus is at its peak.
* **Builder Vitality**: Onboarding includes establishing your personal wellness baseline using the platform (sleep hygiene, circadian alignment, precision nutrition, and ergonomic recovery).

## 🎯 Open Tracks & Fellowship Roles

### 1. Clinical Intelligence & Evidence Fellow (Open / Fellowship)
* **What you’ll do**: Benchmark new clinical care plans against Oxford CEBM (Levels 1–5), Cochrane Risk of Bias 2 (RoB 2), and SPRINT/MIMIC-IV clinical datasets.
* **Who it’s for**: Medical students, resident physicians, nurses, clinical research coordinators, and health informaticians who want to master clinical AI.
* **Skills you’ll gain**: Oxford CEBM appraisal, FHIR R4 standard-of-care architecture, and ONC HTI-2 DSI certification standards.

### 2. Medical ML & Kaggle Engineering Fellow (Open / Fellowship)
* **What you’ll do**: Train and optimize computer vision models (RSNA Knee, ConvNeXt, Swin) and tabular CDS algorithms using leak-free `GroupKFold`, Asymmetric Loss (ASL), and Nelder-Mead threshold optimization.
* **Who it’s for**: Self-taught data scientists, Kaggle competitors, and ML students wanting real-world medical AI experience.
* **Skills you’ll gain**: PyTorch, ONNX runtime, FP16 sub-second inference, DICOM parsing, and Bayesian co-occurrence modeling.

### 3. Full-Stack Web & 3D Spatial UI Engineer (Open / Contract)
* **What you’ll do**: Build accessible, reactive clinical dashboards using Angular 22 Signals, Three.js 3D anatomical models, and WebAssembly / WebGPU offline edge scoring.
* **Who it’s for**: Frontend and full-stack developers passionate about ultra-fast, accessible, and beautiful healthcare tools.
* **Skills you’ll gain**: Angular Signals, WebGPU compute pipelines, Three.js shaders, and WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility.

### 4. Health Equity, Translation & Accessibility Advocate (Open / Micro-Grants)
* **What you’ll do**: Expand cognitive localization (Bionic Reading, simplified clinical translation, multilingual equity) and audit screen-reader accessibility for diverse patient populations.
* **Who it’s for**: Multilingual educators, accessibility advocates, and community health workers.

### 5. Veterans & Military Health Transition Fellow (Open / Priority Fellowship)
* **What you’ll do**: Develop VA Lighthouse FHIR R4 data connectors, PACT Act toxic exposure / burn pit screener algorithms, and TBI somatic recovery protocols.
* **Who it’s for**: Military veterans, combat medics, Navy corpsmen, signals/IT specialists, and military spouses transitioning into high-tech healthcare careers.
* **Skills you’ll gain**: VA Lighthouse API, FHIR R4 Clinical Resources, Agentic AI pairing (Gemini 2.5), and ONC HTI-2 certification.

### 6. Artist-in-Residence & Creative Technologist (Open / Creative Fellow)
* **What you’ll do**: Design clinical 3D anatomical models, WebGL/Three.js shaders, digital art therapy canvases, narrative graphic storybook chapters, and custom glyphs for the open-source PocketGull Typeface family (SIL OFL 1.1).
* **Who it’s for**: Digital painters, medical illustrators, calligraphers, font designers, 3D animators, comic artists, and art therapists.
* **Skills you’ll gain**: Three.js WebGL procedural shaders, Caslon typography engineering, art therapy UX, and narrative medicine design.
