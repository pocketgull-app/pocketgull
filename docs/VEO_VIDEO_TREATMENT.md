### 🛠️ 4. Video Assembly & Production Checklist

1. **Generate Video Clips**: Run the 6 prompt blocks in **Google Veo / Vertex AI Video Studio** (or Google AI Studio Video Lab).
2. **Record Voiceover**: Use Gemini 2.5 Audio / ElevenLabs (Warm, authoritative, empathetic documentary narrator voice).
3. **Screen Recording Inserts (B-Roll)**: Capture high-resolution 60 FPS screen recordings of:
   - Live Gemini voice consult in `AdkLiveComponent`.
   - Rotating Three.js 3D anatomy and organelle viewer in `ZooniverseMicroAnnotationComponent`.
   - Literature research frame and 1-click FHIR R4 PDF export.
4. **Timeline Assembly (DaVinci Resolve / Premiere Pro)**:
   - Layer 1: Veo background cinematic scenes.
   - Layer 2: UI overlay cut-ins with smooth opacity blends.
   - Layer 3: Synchronized voiceover and stereo sound design.
5. **Color Grade**: Apply standard **Kodak 2383 / Clean Teal-Amber Rec.709** LUT with +10% contrast and soft highlight roll-off.
6. **Export**: Master as `ProRes 422 HQ` or `H.264 4K 60Mbps` for YouTube / Devpost embed.
