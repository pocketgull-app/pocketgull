## 🌲 Phase 2: Claim Oregon Business Matching Grants ($50,000–$100,000)

The State of Oregon runs the **Oregon Innovation Council (Oregon InC)** through **Business Oregon**:

1. **SBIR Support Program**: Oregon provides free grant counseling, professional technical review, and grant-writing stipends to Oregon companies preparing SBIR proposals.
2. **Phase 0 & Matching Grants**: When your federal proposal is submitted or awarded, Oregon matches it with up to **$100,000 in state cash**.
3. **Contact Portal**: Visit **[https://www.oregon.gov/biz/programs/SBIR-STTR/](https://www.oregon.gov/biz/programs/SBIR-STTR/)** or email `SBIR-Support@biz.oregon.gov`.
