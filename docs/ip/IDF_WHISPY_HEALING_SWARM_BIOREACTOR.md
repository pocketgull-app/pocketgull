## 1. ABSTRACT
Disclosed is a closed-loop volumetric bio-fabrication containment apparatus ("Bioreactor Tank") and associated supramolecular biomaterial system for synthesizing, acoustic-sculpting, and deploying ephemeral regenerative healing swarms directly from multi-dimensional patient medical scans (e.g., DICOM CT/MRI or optical stereophotogrammetry). The system comprises an environmentally isolated sterile containment chamber housing a multi-axis ultrasonic phased array ($40\text{--}250\text{ kHz}$) and piezoelectric micro-mesh nebulizers. Liquid-liquid phase-separated (LLPS) peptide coacervate droplets carrying synthetic exosome payloads are aerosolized into the chamber, where three-dimensional acoustic radiation pressure nodes—derived via inverted Gor'kov potential field algorithms from patient scan geometry—levitate and sculpt the mist in mid-air into an anatomically tailored porous regenerative matrix. Upon thermal and ionic exposure ($\text{Ca}^{2+} \ge 1.2\text{ mM}$, $T \ge 34^\circ\text{C}$), the suspended coacervates undergo an instantaneous sol-to-gel transition, crosslinking into a conductive nanofibrillar hydrogel ($0.1\text{--}1.5\text{ S/m}$) that restores the endogenous transepithelial bioelectric current of injury ($40\text{--}100\text{ mV/mm}$) to accelerate galvanotaxic re-epithelialization without scarring.

## 2. TECHNICAL FIELD & CLINICAL BACKGROUND

### 2.1 The Clinical Dilemmas in Regenerative Wound Closure
In severe tissue trauma, third-degree burns, diabetic foot ulcers (DFUs), and complex surgical resections, contemporary medicine relies on two unsatisfactory extremes:
1. **Solid Pre-Formed Grafts & Dressings**: Collagen sheets (e.g., Integra, bovine/porcine xenografts) and synthetic films are planar, rigid, and cannot conform seamlessly to irregular, deep, undermined micro-fissures or friable internal parenchyma, leaving dead space where seromas and anaerobic bacterial biofilms (*Pseudomonas*, *S. aureus*) flourish.
2. **Topical Liquid Glues & Sprays**: Fibrin glues and cyanoacrylates provide immediate hemostasis but polymerize into non-porous, brittle plastic barriers that block cellular infiltration, trigger thermal necrosis, and cause excessive fibrotic scarring (keloids) while lacking active paracrine regenerative signals.

### 2.2 Shortcomings of the Prior Art Disclaimed Herein
* **Samuel Stupp et al. (Peptide Amphiphiles)**: While self-assembling peptide amphiphiles form hydrogels in bulk liquid, existing approaches require physical syringe extrusion or manual brush application, causing mechanical shear damage to friable wound edges and failing to provide three-dimensional spatial patterning prior to tissue contact.
* **Acoustic Levitation (Ultrahaptics / Bristol)**: Prior acoustic levitation systems have been used for non-contact droplet transport or tactile haptics, but have never been integrated into a hermetic sterile containment bioreactor that couples real-time DICOM scan inversion with in-flight sol-to-gel phase transitions and bioelectric polarization.

## 4. DETAILED CHEMICAL & ARCHITECTURAL DESCRIPTION

### 4.1 Chemical Formula & Markush Structures (Pillar 1)
The whispy healing matrix is synthesized from a tri-block supramolecular peptide amphiphile conjugate having the general structural formula:

$$\mathbf{R}_1 - \left[ \text{Hydrophobic Tail} \right] - \left[ \text{MMP Cleavable Linker} \right] - \left[ \text{Bio-Conductive Moiety} \right] - \left[ \text{Charged Headgroup} \right] - \mathbf{R}_2$$

Wherein:
* **Hydrophobic Tail**: A saturated or mono-unsaturated alkyl acyl chain selected from $\text{C}_{12}\text{H}_{25}\text{CO}-$ (lauryl), $\text{C}_{15}\text{H}_{31}\text{CO}-$ (palmitoyl), or $\text{C}_{17}\text{H}_{35}\text{CO}-$ (stearoyl).
* **MMP Cleavable Linker**: A hexapeptide sequence sensitive to Matrix Metalloproteinase-2 and Matrix Metalloproteinase-9 cleavage, selected from the group consisting of:
  * $\text{Gly-Pro-Gln-Gly-Ile-Trp-Gly-Gln}$
  * $\text{Gly-Pro-Leu-Gly-Ile-Ala-Gly-Gln}$
  * $\text{Pro-Leu-Gly-Met-Trp-Ser-Arg}$
* **Bio-Conductive Moiety**: An electroactive conjugated oligomer selected from:
  * Doped poly(3,4-ethylenedioxythiophene):poly(styrenesulfonate) (PEDOT:PSS) oligomers ($n = 3\text{--}8$).
  * Oligomeric polydopamine-melanin zwitterionic adducts.
  * Carboxylated graphene/carbon nanodots ($< 2.5\text{ nm}$ diameter).
* **Charged Headgroup**: A hydrophilic peptide sequence capable of undergoing electrostatic screening in the presence of divalent cations ($\text{Ca}^{2+}$, $\text{Mg}^{2+}$), selected from $\text{Glu-Glu-Glu-Asp-Asp}$ (anionic) or $\text{Arg-Arg-Lys-Lys}$ (cationic).
* **Synthetic Exosome Cargo**: Enclosed within the liquid-liquid phase-separated core at a density of $10^9\text{--}10^{11}\text{ particles/mL}$, comprising:
  * **microRNA-146a & microRNA-21**: To downregulate NF-$\kappa\text{B}$ and extinguish inflammatory TNF-$\alpha$/IL-6.
  * **Recombinant Human TGF-$\beta 3$**: To induce organized, parallel collagen III deposition mimicking fetal scarless wound architecture while suppressing fibrotic TGF-$\beta 1$.
  * **Vascular Endothelial Growth Factor 165 (VEGF-A)**: Stabilized with heparan sulfate proteoglycan mimetic for continuous 72-hour angiogenic capillary budding.

1. **Isolated Containment Tank (100)**: A cylindrical or hexagonal borosilicate glass or titanium Grade 5 reaction vessel ($10\text{--}35\text{ cm}$ diameter, $15\text{--}50\text{ cm}$ height), rated for positive pressure and high-vacuum autoclave sterilization.
2. **Top and Bottom Annular Ultrasonic Arrays (104, 114)**: Opposing rings of Lead Zirconate Titanate (PZT) ultrasonic transducers driven at $40\text{--}250\text{ kHz}$ with independently adjustable per-channel phase shifts ($\Delta \phi \in [0, 2\pi]$) generating volumetric standing waves.
3. **Piezoelectric Micro-Mesh Nebulizer Ports (106)**: Dual oscillating membrane nebulizers (aperture diameter $1.8\text{ }\mu\text{m}$, oscillation frequency $110\text{ kHz}$) providing laminar, monodisperse droplet delivery without shearing fragile supramolecular peptide chains.
4. **Secondary Crosslinker Atomizer (108)**: High-pressure micro-orifice nozzle delivering heated ($37^\circ\text{C}$) physiological saline containing $2.5\text{--}5.0\text{ mM }\text{CaCl}_2$ as an aerosolized gas-phase trigger.
5. **Stereoscopic Tyndall Light-Scattering Reticle (110)**: Dual orthogonally aligned $532\text{ nm}$ solid-state green lasers with high-speed CMOS sensors detecting Rayleigh and Mie scattering to compute in-flight droplet spatial density and 3D boundary profile.
6. **Bioelectric Bias Field Plates (112)**: Platinum-iridium transparent wire mesh electrodes applying an adjustable electrostatic gradient ($40\text{--}100\text{ mV/mm}$) across the acoustic node to pre-orient the conjugated conductive polymers prior to gelation.
7. **Sterile Canister Docking Port (116)**: Hermetic valve interface permitting either vacuum-assisted canisterization into a handheld surgical applicator or automated transfer to an incubation tray.

### 4.3 Scan-to-Acoustic Inversion & Manufacturing Method (Pillar 3)

The 5-phase closed-loop transformation executes as follows:

```
[ Phase 1: Scan Voxelization ]
    Patient DICOM CT / 3D Surface Mesh
    ───► Spatial Voxel Bounding Box V(x, y, z)
    ───► Gor'kov Potential Inversion: U_acoustic(r) = 2πr³ ρ₀ [ ⟨p²⟩/(3ρ₀²c₀²) f₁ - ⟨v²⟩/2 f₂ ]
    ───► Generate Phase-Delay Vector {φ₁, φ₂, ..., φ₁₂₈} for Transducer Array

[ Phase 2: Colloidal Mist Inoculation ]
    Nebulize peptide coacervate solution at 0.5 mL/min into chamber
    Chamber environment: 22°C, 95% RH, sterile N₂/Air blanket

[ Phase 3: Volumetric Acoustic Holographic Sculpting ]
    Energize Transducers at computed phase matrix {φ_k}
    Acoustic radiation forces trap droplets into nodal surfaces corresponding to V(x, y, z)
    Tyndall camera validates geometric fidelity (tolerance < 50 μm)

[ Phase 4: In-Flight Sol-to-Gel Flash Crosslinking ]
    Inject 37°C atomized CaCl₂ micro-vapor
    Electrostatic screening triggers instant β-sheet nanofibrillar self-assembly
    Mist solidifies into flexible, porous, high-surface-area biopolymer matrix

[ Phase 5: Bioelectric Polarization & Sterile Harvesting ]
    Energize Pt/Ir bias electrodes at 80 mV/mm for 10 seconds to align dipole domains
    Depressurize chamber / transfer matrix to sterile surgical cartridge or directly to wound
```

## 5. EXEMPLIFIED PROVISIONAL PATENT CLAIMS

### Independent Claim 1 (Apparatus):
> An acoustic holographic volumetric bio-fabrication apparatus for synthesizing an anatomically contoured supramolecular regenerative matrix, comprising:  
> (a) an environmentally sealed sterile containment vessel defining an interior reaction volume;  
> (b) an acoustic transducer array comprising a plurality of ultrasonic emitters disposed circumferentially about said containment vessel;  
> (c) a primary nebulizer port configured to aerosolize a liquid-liquid phase-separated peptide coacervate solution into said interior volume;  
> (d) a secondary nebulizer port configured to deliver an aerosolized ionic crosslinking agent into said interior volume;  
> (e) a bioelectric polarization system comprising a plurality of electrodes configured to establish a directed electrical field across said interior volume; and  
> (f) a closed-loop controller communicably coupled to said transducer array, said nebulizers, and a medical imaging dataset, said controller configured to execute instructions causing said transducer array to project a three-dimensional acoustic radiation pressure field that levitates and sculpts said aerosolized coacervates into a defined three-dimensional anatomical geometry corresponding to said imaging dataset prior to ionic crosslinking.

### Independent Claim 8 (Composition of Matter):
> An aerosolized supramolecular regenerative composition, comprising:  
> a plurality of liquid-liquid phase-separated colloidal coacervate droplets having an aerodynamic diameter between 1.0 and 3.0 microns, each droplet comprising:  
> (i) an amphiphilic oligopeptide conjugate having an alkyl hydrophobic tail, an enzymatic matrix metalloproteinase-cleavable sequence, and a charged hydrophilic terminus;  
> (ii) an electroactive conductive polymer moiety covalently linked to said oligopeptide; and  
> (iii) a payload of synthetic exosomes encapsulated within said droplet;  
> wherein upon exposure to an aerosolized physiological buffer containing calcium ions at greater than 1.2 mM and a temperature of at least 34 degrees Celsius, said droplets undergo an instantaneous sol-to-gel transition to form an interconnected porous nanofibrillar hydrogel possessing an electrical conductivity between 0.1 and 1.5 S/m.

### Independent Claim 15 (Method of Treatment):
> A method of accelerating tissue regeneration and re-epithelialization in a cutaneous or parenchymal tissue defect, comprising:  
> (a) scanning said tissue defect to generate a three-dimensional defect topology dataset;  
> (b) volumetric-sculpting an aerosolized mist of conductive peptide coacervates using acoustic radiation forces into a three-dimensional geometry matching said defect topology dataset inside an isolated containment bioreactor;  
> (c) triggering in-flight gelation of said coacervates to form an anatomically matched bio-conductive regenerative scaffold; and  
> (d) depositing said scaffold into said tissue defect, whereby said bio-conductive scaffold restores an endogenous lateral transepithelial electric field of 40 to 100 mV/mm to direct galvanotaxic migration of host epidermal cells across said defect without keloid scarring.
