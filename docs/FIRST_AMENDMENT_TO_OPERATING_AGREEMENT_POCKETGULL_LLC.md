# FIRST AMENDMENT TO THE OPERATING AGREEMENT OF POCKETGULL LLC
**An Oregon Limited Liability Company**

This **FIRST AMENDMENT TO THE OPERATING AGREEMENT** of **PocketGull LLC** (the "Company"), an Oregon limited liability company organized under the Oregon Limited Liability Company Act (ORS Chapter 63), is adopted and entered into effective as of **September 25, 2026**, by **Phillip Gear**, being the Sole Member holding one hundred percent (100%) of the Membership and Voting Interests of the Company (the "Sole Member").

---

### RECITALS

**WHEREAS**, the Company entered into that certain Operating Agreement of PocketGull LLC (the "Original Operating Agreement"), establishing the governance, management, and membership rights of the Company;

**WHEREAS**, pursuant to **Section 4.1(C)(iv)** and **Section 10.2** of the Original Operating Agreement, the Operating Agreement may be modified or amended in writing signed by all of the Members;

**WHEREAS**, the Sole Member desires to amend the Original Operating Agreement to permanently enshrine core stewardship covenants, anti-enclosure protections against predatory financial takeovers, strict zero-egress HIPAA Safe Harbor data sovereignty, and authority to federate with public health commons pursuant to Elinor Ostrom's governing principles;

**NOW, THEREFORE**, the Sole Member hereby amends the Original Operating Agreement by adding the following Articles:

---

### ARTICLE 11: PUBLIC HEALTH COMMONS STEWARDSHIP & ANTI-ENCLOSURE INVARIANT

**11.1 Stewardship Purpose & Non-Alienation.** The primary clinical mission of the Company is to provide open, accessible, high-legibility clinical decision support, sovereign health tools, and evidence-grounded health strategies to individuals, independent clinicians, Federally Qualified Health Centers (FQHCs), and underserved communities without economic exclusion.

**11.2 Prohibition of Predatory Enclosure & Private Equity Acquisition.** The Company, its managers, officers, and members are expressly prohibited from entering into any merger, sale, consolidation, transfer of all or substantially all assets, or equity restructuring with any private equity firm, speculative debt fund, real estate investment trust (REIT), or predatory acquirer whose business model involves:
1. Asset-stripping, closure, or debt-leveraging of community health facilities or educational commons;
2. Imposing artificial paywalls, subscription tolls, or closed proprietary locks on the Company’s core open-access clinical triage utilities and open HL7 FHIR standards; or
3. Liquidating or encumbering the intellectual property of the Company to satisfy external speculative debt obligations.

**11.3 Public Domain / Copyleft Fallback (Poison Pill).** In the event of any involuntary bankruptcy, hostile creditor foreclosure, or legal attempt by any external party to enclose or monopolize the Company's clinical algorithms, the source code, clinical schemas, and decision engines shall immediately and irrevocably convert to the public domain under an un-encumbered open-source copyleft license (AGPL-3.0 or equivalent) to guarantee permanent availability to the public commons.

---

### ARTICLE 12: DATA SOVEREIGNTY, ZERO-EGRESS PRIVACY & HIPAA COMPLIANCE

**12.1 Zero-Egress Architecture.** All software, companion tools, and algorithmic systems developed by the Company shall maintain a zero-egress, client-side, on-device computing posture by design. Electronic Protected Health Information (ePHI) shall remain strictly within the physical custody and sovereign control of the patient or treating clinician.

**12.2 Absolute Prohibition on Data Commercialization.** The Company shall never sell, monetize, broker, exchange, or transfer patient health records, biometric signatures, cycle-tracking logs, or clinical telemetry to any commercial data broker, advertiser, insurer, or third-party artificial intelligence foundation model training pipeline.

**12.3 Cryptographic Integrity (FDA 21 CFR Part 11 & NIST SP 800-90A).** All clinical data serialization and attestation generated by the Company's software shall utilize NIST SP 800-90A compliant CSPRNG hardware entropy and immutable SHA-256 digital provenance seals to ensure non-repudiation and prevent data tampering.

---

### ARTICLE 13: ELINOR OSTROM COMMONS FEDERATION

**13.1 Authority to Federate.** The Company is explicitly authorized to enter into bilateral and multilateral federation covenants with independent community health clinics, rural midwifery networks, Community Land Trusts (CLTs), municipal tool guilds, and traditional knowledge trusts.

**13.2 Adherence to Ostrom Core Invariants.** All federation alliances entered into by the Company shall conform to Elinor Ostrom's Eight Design Principles for Common Pool Resources (CPRs), ensuring clearly defined resource boundaries, proportional sweat-equity reciprocity, direct collective choice, accountable peer monitoring, graduated restorative sanctions, fast low-cost conflict resolution, recognized rights to self-determine, and nested polycentric enterprise structures.

---

### ARTICLE 14: RATIFICATION & FULL FORCE

Except as expressly amended hereby, the Original Operating Agreement shall remain in full force and effect in all respects. In the event of any direct conflict between the terms of this First Amendment and the Original Operating Agreement, the terms of this First Amendment shall control.
