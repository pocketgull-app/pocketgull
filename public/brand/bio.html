In a circle where empathy grows,
And care like a mountain stream flows,
He drew with kind ink
To help everyone think,
And soothe tired eyes from their woes!

Phil Gear is an American graphic designer, computer scientist, and photographer based in the Pacific Northwest, and the founder of GearArts. Guided by a deep belief in diversity, empathy, and universal accessibility, Gear engineered PocketGull as an open-source clinical sans-serif, display, and telemetry monospace superfamily. The design unites tactile humanist warmth with ISO tactile Braille, Louise Sloan 5:1 optotypes, and ISMP medication safety standards to bring clarity, fatigue-reduction, and comfort to caregivers and patients worldwide.
