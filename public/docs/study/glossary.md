## 🔬 Clinical Paradigms & Systems

### Allopathic Neuroscience & Medicine
- [Allopathic Medicine](https://snomed.org "[Clinical Paradigm] Western evidence-based medicine focusing on pathophysiological mechanisms, ICD-10 diagnostic codes, SNOMED CT terminology, and clinical laboratory vitals."): Conventional Western medical science based on empirical clinical trials, biochemistry, and anatomical pathology.
- [Autonomic Nervous System](https://ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/mesh/68001341 "[Neuroscience] Division of the nervous system regulating involuntary physiological functions including Heart Rate Variability (HRV), Blood Pressure, and Vagal Tone."): Controls involuntary bodily functions (sympathetic fight-or-flight vs parasympathetic rest-and-digest).
- [FHIR R4 Bundle](https://hl7.org/fhir/R4/ "[Health Interoperability] HL7 Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources standard format for exporting patient electronic health record bundles."): Standardized JSON payload format for seamless electronic health record exchange between hospital systems.

### Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM)
- [Wu Xing Five Elements](https://ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4231644/ "[TCM Paradigm] Traditional Chinese Medicine framework categorizing organ networks and natural relationships into Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, and Water."): Interconnected elemental cycle governing Zang-Fu organ systems and seasonal balance.
- [Meridian Pathways](https://tcm.ac.cn "[TCM Acupuncture] Energetic channels carrying Qi and Blood through the body, associated with specific acupuncture points (e.g. LV-3 Taichong, HT-7 Shenmen)."): 14 primary pathways through which Qi flows, mapped in Pocket-Gull's 3D Body Viewer.
- **Zang-Fu Organ Clock**: Diurnal organ energetic peak timing matrix.

### Ayurvedic Medicine & Marma Science
- [Tridosha](https://ayurveda.com "[Ayurvedic Paradigm] Three fundamental bio-energetic forces in Ayurveda: Vata (Air/Space), Pitta (Fire/Water), and Kapha (Earth/Water)."): Bio-elemental constitutions determining individual physiological and mental tendencies.
- [Marma Points](https://ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3611634/ "[Veda Science] 107 vital anatomical intersections of muscles, veins, ligaments, bones, and joints carrying Pranic life force energy."): Vital energy points targeted in Ayurvedic acupressure and spatial 3D mapping.
