## 🏛️ Preamble: Beyond Defensive Legalese

Health intelligence belongs to humanity. Technology must serve the flourishing of the human vessel.

## 🛡️ Pillar 2: Bio-Sovereignty & The Edge Privacy Covenant

1. **Biological Data Ownership**: You own your telemetry, voice acoustic biomarkers, and clinical history.
2. **Zero Third-Party Data Monetization**: Pocketgull will never sell, license, or trade patient data to advertising networks, insurance underwriters, or data brokers.
3. **1-Click Ephemeral Master Purge**: Patients retain the absolute right to purge all transient local state and telemetry with a single click (`purgeTransientPatientState`).
4. **Client-Side Edge Computation ("Shifting Left")**: Acoustic FFT pitch analysis, HRV telemetry math, and biophysical equations execute locally on the client's device via WebAssembly/WebGPU.
