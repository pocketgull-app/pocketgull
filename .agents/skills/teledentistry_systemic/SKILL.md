# Teledentistry and Systemic Health Cross-Talk Skill

This skill provides domain instructions for building and auditing teledentistry, odontogram surfaces, and systemic periodontitis-cardiovascular cross-talk features in PocketGull.

---

## 1. FDI 32-Tooth Odontogram Grid (Teeth 11–48)

* Teeth are identified using standard **FDI International Notation** (Quadrants 1–4).
* Caries & Restorations are mapped to 5 anatomical surfaces:
  * **M**: Mesial
  * **O**: Occlusal
  * **D**: Distal
  * **F**: Facial / Buccal
  * **L**: Lingual / Palatal

---

## 2. Smith & Knight Tooth Wear Index (TWI Grades 0–4)

* **Grade 0**: Normal enamel contour.
* **Grade 1**: Superficial smooth wear facets (enamel).
* **Grade 2**: Dentin exposure $< 1/3$ surface area.
* **Grade 3**: Severe dentin loss $> 1/3$ surface area.
* **Grade 4**: Complete pulp chamber exposure.

---

## 3. Systemic Inflammatory Burden Index (SIBI 0–100)

Calculated combining periodontal probing depth ($\text{PPD} \ge 4\text{ mm}$), Bleeding-on-Probing ($\%BOP$), and High-Sensitivity C-Reactive Protein ($\text{hs-CRP}$):

$$\text{SIBI} = \min\left(100, (\text{Deep Pockets} \times 6) + (\%BOP \times 0.8) + (\text{hs-CRP} \times 12)\right)$$

* **Cardiovascular Risk Multiplier**: Range $1.0x\text{--}2.8x$ based on SIBI and systemic trans-epithelial bacteremia ($P. gingivalis$).
* **Predicted HbA1c Elevation**: $+0.0\%\text{--}+0.8\%$ driven by systemic cytokine ($\text{TNF-}\alpha, \text{IL-6}$) insulin receptor resistance.
